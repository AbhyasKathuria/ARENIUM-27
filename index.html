ABOUT THE FEST

The Arena of Innovation // Annual National Inter-College Tech Fest

ARENIUM'27 is the premier annual national tech festival presented by the Presidency School of Information Science (PSIS / SOIS) at Presidency University, Bengaluru. Designed as a high-octane proving ground under the theme "The Arena of Innovation", Arenium convenes coders, innovators, developers, gamers, and tech visionaries from across the nation to test their intellect and ignite digital transformation.

Taking place on 4–5 February 2027 at the Presidency University campus, the fest features 10 dynamic competitive arenas including the flagship 24-Hour Hackathon, Blind Coding (Code in the Dark), Technical Treasure Hunt (Tech Trail), UI/UX DesignX, Data to Deployment (Data Tales), Prompt to Prototype, Startup Pitchthon (TechTalk), Gaming Arena (Valorant, BGMI, COD), Arenium Premier League (APL), and the Techno-Organic Synthesis Fashion Show.
