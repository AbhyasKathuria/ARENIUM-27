ABOUT THE FESTIVAL

Where cutting-edge technology converges with collegiate creativity and competition.

ARENIUM'27 is the premier annual national tech festival presented by the School of Information Science (SOIS) at Presidency University, Bengaluru. Designed as a high-octane proving ground, Arenium brings together coders, gamers, designers, innovators, and tech visionaries from across the nation to test their intellect and ignite digital transformation.

Immerse yourself in two adrenaline-fueled days of competitive coding battles, strategic LAN esports arenas, live cryptic treasure hunts, robotics and tech quizzes, creative design showdowns, and hands-on workshops guided by industry stalwarts. Whether you come to claim victory in the arenas or collaborate on revolutionary ideas, Arenium'27 is your portal to the next frontier of computing.
