5. Points to Confirm

Headcount: this plan uses 19 faculty + 40 students = 59 members, so the proposal’s figures (36 faculty + 70 students = 118, which actually sums to 106) need updating.

Small team: ThinkTank and TechVerse finish on Day 1, while Venture Arena and Pixel Showdown run only on Day 2, so their coordinators can help with registration and hall duties on the other day.
Food budget: ₹66,000 may not cover two days of tea and lunch for ~600 people.
Venue table: update the requirements table to 4 seminar halls and 1 auditorium.

ByteBlitz overnight: teams leave campus overnight; confirm this is allowed or end the sprint on Day 1.
Old Prayer Hall (4th floor): confirm capacity, power points, Wi-Fi coverage, lift or stair access, and that the hall is cleared and available for ByteBlitz on both days.
Stalls: the ₹20,000 stall revenue needs space outside these five venues, such as the auditorium foyer.
